NGRIP ice-core oxygen isotopes, 20-year means, GICC05 timescale

File:      ngrip_d18o_20yr.csv (5638 rows, comma-separated, header row)
Columns:   age_b2k      age in years before 2000 CE (b2k), GICC05 timescale, 20-year steps
           d18O_permil  d18O of the ice, per mil (VSMOW), 20-year mean
Range:     9,540 to 122,280 years b2k. Larger (less negative) d18O = warmer Greenland.
Events:    GICC05 ages of the transitions used in the group tasks (Rasmussen et al., 2014):
           Holocene onset (end of the Younger Dryas)  11,703 b2k (counting uncertainty +-99 yr)
           Younger Dryas onset                        12,896 b2k (+-138 yr)
           Bolling onset (GI-1e)                      14,692 b2k (+-186 yr)
Processing: the supplied file was a step-plot export with every value listed twice;
           duplicate ages were removed, leaving one row per 20-year bin.
Source:    NGRIP members (2004), Nature 431, 147-151; Rasmussen et al. (2014), Quat. Sci. Rev. 106, 14-28;
           Seierstad et al. (2014), Quat. Sci. Rev. 106, 29-46. Original data distributed by the Centre for Ice and Climate,
           University of Copenhagen (www.iceandclimate.nbi.ku.dk). This copy was supplied by the course
           director; the download date was not recorded.
Used in:   practicals/03_methods (group tasks, Task 2).
