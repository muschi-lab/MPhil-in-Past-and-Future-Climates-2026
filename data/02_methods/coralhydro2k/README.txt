CoralHydro2k v1.0.1: coral d18O and Sr/Ca records, LiPD format

Files:     179 .lpd files, one per coral site or core (about 1.6 MB). Unmodified copy.
Variables: d18O (per mil, VPDB), Sr/Ca (mmol/mol), time (year CE), latitude, longitude, ocean.
Used in:   practicals/02_methods (main notebook and group tasks).

Source:    https://lipdverse.org/CoralHydro2k/
Citation:  Walter, R. M., et al. (2023). The CoralHydro2k database. Earth System Science
           Data, 15, 2081-2116. https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-15-2081-2023
           For one record, also cite the original paper listed inside its file.
Note:      Series named d18O include a few reconstructed seawater d18O series (names end in _sw).
